Privacy Policy

📅 Last Updated: October 2026 🔒 Google Play & COPPA Compliant

🌟 Family & Child Safety Commitment: Bubble Blast Saga is designed for players of all ages. We do NOT collect, transmit, or sell sensitive personal information. We strictly comply with Google Play Families Policy, COPPA, and international data privacy regulations.

1. Overview & Developer Identification

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile game Bubble Blast Saga (Package Name: com.bubbleblast.saga), developed and published by Vishal Lingayat ("we", "us", or "our").

If you have questions or concerns about this policy or your privacy rights, you can reach out directly via email at vlingayat0924@gmail.com.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

To respect your privacy to the highest standard, our game operates with zero unnecessary permissions:

  • No Real-Name or Identity: We never ask for your real name, phone number, address, or physical location.
  • No Sensitive Hardware Access: We never request access to your microphone, camera, contacts, phone calls, or calendar.
  • No Precise GPS Location: We never track your precise GPS coordinates.

3. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

The game saves your progress directly to your device's local storage (using Android SharedPreferences). This includes:

  • Current level progress, high scores, and stars achieved (Levels 1–200+).
  • Virtual in-game coin and gem balances.
  • Current heart / life counts and timer timestamps.
  • Inventory of power-up boosters (Bombs, Fireballs, Rainbows, Aim line, Paint).
  • Player audio preferences (Music on/off, Sound FX on/off, Haptic feedback).

This data stays on your local device and is never uploaded to personal profiling databases.

4. Third-Party Services & SDK Disclosures

To maintain game stability, deliver optional video rewards, and provide secure purchases, Bubble Blast Saga integrates industry-standard third-party SDKs provided by Google and Unity:

A. Google Firebase Crashlytics & Analytics

We use Firebase Crashlytics and Firebase Analytics to monitor crash frequency and ensure silky-smooth performance across various Android devices.

  • Data Collected: Pseudonymous device identifiers, operating system version, device model, crash stack traces, and anonymous in-game event counts (e.g., level completed).
  • Purpose: Identifying and fixing bugs and crashes rapidly.
  • Google Firebase Privacy Policy

B. Google AdMob & Google Mobile Ads

Bubble Blast Saga displays non-intrusive banner, interstitial, and rewarded video advertisements via Google AdMob and the Google Mobile Ads SDK.

  • Families Compliance: In accordance with Google Play Families guidelines, ad requests are configured with family-safe rating flags. We do not transmit behavioral targeting data for child-directed sessions.
  • Google Advertising Privacy Policy

C. Unity Ads Mediation

Unity Ads is integrated via AdMob Mediation to serve high-quality opt-in rewarded video advertisements (for example, allowing players to watch a short clip to revive extra moves or refill lives).

  • Unity Privacy Policy

D. Google Play In-App Billing

All digital transactions (such as coin packs and booster bundles) are processed directly through Google Play Billing.

  • We never receive, process, or store your credit card, debit card, or banking details. All financial transactions are managed entirely by Google's secure payment infrastructure.
  • Google Payments Privacy Notice

5. Children's Online Privacy Protection (COPPA & Families Policy)

We are committed to preserving children's privacy. Bubble Blast Saga:

  • Does not knowingly solicit or collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area).
  • Does not display age-inappropriate advertisements or unverified external content.
  • If a parent or guardian discovers that a child has inadvertently provided personal information, please contact us at vlingayat0924@gmail.com, and we will promptly delete it.

6. Data Retention & User Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

Under regulations such as GDPR and CCPA, you have the right to:

  • Access & Deletion: You can clear all game data at any time by navigating to your device's Settings > Apps > Bubble Blast Saga > Clear Storage / Cache, or by uninstalling the app.
  • Opt-Out of Personalized Ads: You can reset or delete your Android Advertising ID at any time via Settings > Google > Ads.
  • Contact Us: You may submit any data privacy inquiries or deletion requests by contacting us directly at vlingayat0924@gmail.com.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect app updates or regulatory modifications. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
