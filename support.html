Troubleshooting Guide

Quick Fixes for Common Questions

My lives are not regenerating. How can I fix this? +
Lives regenerate automatically based on real-world time (1 life every 30 minutes). Please ensure your device's date and time are set to "Automatic" (Network-provided time). Modifying your system clock forward or backward may disrupt the life countdown timer.
I bought a booster pack or coins but didn't receive them. +
Ensure you have a stable internet connection. Restart the app; our Google Play Billing listener automatically synchronizes and delivers any pending unconsumed purchases on startup. If still missing, send your Google Play order ID to vlingayat0924@gmail.com and we will manually credit your items immediately.
The game has no sound or music. +
Check the in-game Settings menu (gear icon in the top right). Ensure both "Sound" and "Music" toggles are switched ON. Also check your phone's media volume (not just ringtone volume) and make sure "Do Not Disturb" mode is disabled.
How do I clear or reset my game data? +
To start over from Level 1, go to your phone's Settings > Apps > Bubble Blast Saga > Storage > Clear Storage / Clear Data. Please note this permanently resets your level progress, coin balance, and boosters.
