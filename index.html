Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers regarding gameplay, offline mode, and in-game economy.

Is Bubble Blast Saga completely free to play? +
Yes! Bubble Blast Saga is 100% free to download and play. All levels can be beaten with skill and strategy. Optional in-game coins, gems, and booster packs can be purchased through Google Play Billing.
Can I play without an active internet connection? +
Absolutely! All 200+ puzzles are preloaded on your device. You can play completely offline anytime, anywhere. Internet is only required for optional rewarded video boosts and store purchases.
How do Lives (Hearts) recharge? +
You start with a maximum of 5 lives. If you run out of moves on a level, you lose 1 life. Lives naturally recharge 1 heart every 30 minutes. You can also instantly refill all 5 lives using gems or by watching a short rewarded video!
What should I do if I change phones or lose my progress? +
Your level progress, coin balance, and boosters are securely saved to your device. If you encounter any issues or need assistance, reach out to our team at vlingayat0924@gmail.com.
